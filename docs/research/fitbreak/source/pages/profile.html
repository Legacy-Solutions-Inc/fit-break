🚀 Getting Started

⭐ Quick Start Guide (Read This First!)

Welcome to FitBreak! Here's how to get started in 3 easy steps:

1

Buy Credits

Go to Pricing → Choose a package → Pay via GCash/BPI → Wait for approval

2

Book a Class

Go to Schedule → Pick a class → Click Book Now → Done!

3

Show Up & Sweat!

Arrive 10 mins early → Check in with instructor → Enjoy your workout!

📅 How to Book a Class
  1. Go to Schedule page
  2. Browse available classes by day
  3. Click on a class to view details
  4. Click "Book Now" to reserve your spot
  5. You'll receive an email confirmation

Note: You need credits to book classes. Each class costs 1 credit.

💳 How to Buy Credits
  1. Go to Pricing page
  2. Choose a package that suits your needs
  3. Click "Get Started" on your preferred package
  4. Select payment method (GCash or BPI)
  5. Send payment to the displayed account
  6. Upload screenshot of your payment
  7. Wait for admin approval (usually 1-2 hours)

Available Packages:

  • • Drop-In: 1 credit (7 days validity)
  • • Starter Pack: 6 credits (30 days)
  • • Standard Pack: 12 credits (30 days)
  • • Power Pack: 20 credits (45 days)
✅ How to Check In to a Class

Three easy ways to check in:

📲

Option 1: Scan the Studio QR (Fastest!)

  1. Look for the QR code displayed at the studio counter
  2. Open your phone camera and scan it
  3. Tap the link that appears on your screen
  4. Sign in (if needed) and tap your class to check in

💡 Self check-in is available 30 min before to 15 min after class

📱

Option 2: Show Your Booking QR

  1. Go to My Bookings before class
  2. Find your upcoming class - you'll see a QR code
  3. Show it to Coach Iren when you arrive
  4. She'll scan it and you're checked in!

💡 Your QR code appears 24 hours before class

🙋

Option 3: Manual Check-in

  1. Tell Coach Iren your name when you arrive
  2. She'll find your booking and check you in

Tip: You can view your booked classes in your My Bookings.

❌ How to Cancel a Booking
  1. Go to My Bookings
  2. Find the class you want to cancel
  3. Click "Cancel" on the booking
  4. Your credit will be refunded automatically

Cancellation Policy: Cancel at least 4 hours before the class to get your credit back. Late cancellations may forfeit the credit.

💳 Payments & Credits

🎫 Understanding Your Credits

What are credits?

Credits are your class passes. 1 credit = 1 class session. Buy them in packages and use them to book classes.

Where to see your credits:

  • • Dashboard: Shows remaining credits prominently
  • • Sidebar: Quick view on desktop
  • • Profile page: Detailed credit info

⏰ Credit Expiry

Credits expire based on your package validity (7-45 days). Check your dashboard to see when your credits expire. Use them before they're gone!

📊 Tracking Your Payment Status

Payment Status Guide:

Pending Waiting for admin verification (usually 1-2 hours)
Approved Payment verified! Credits added to your account
Rejected Issue with payment - check your email for details
Refunded Money returned to your account

Check your payment history in the Payments page.

ADMIN Administrator Guide
💰 Approving Payments
  1. Go to Admin Dashboard > Payments tab
  2. Review pending payments (yellow status)
  3. Click "View Receipt" to verify the payment screenshot
  4. Check that the amount matches the package price
  5. Click "Approve" to add credits to the user
  6. User will receive an email confirmation

If payment is suspicious: Click "Reject" and select the appropriate reason (Unclear Proof, Wrong Amount, Wrong Account, or Fraudulent). The user will be notified accordingly.

🔄 Processing Refunds
  1. Find the approved payment in the Payments tab
  2. Click "Refund" button (blue)
  3. Select refund method (GCash, BPI, Cash)
  4. Enter the reference number from your refund transaction
  5. Credits will be automatically deducted from the user
  6. User receives email notification of refund

Note: Always process the actual refund via GCash/BPI before marking as refunded in the system.

✅ Checking In Members
  1. Go to Admin Dashboard > Bookings tab
  2. Find the member's booking for today's class
  3. Click "Check In" when they arrive
  4. System will verify they have credits
  5. If no credits, you'll be prompted to redirect to Credits page

Important: Members with 0 credits cannot be checked in. They must purchase a package first.

🎫 Managing Credits Manually
  1. Go to Credits Management
  2. Search for the user by email or name
  3. Click "Add Credits" or "Remove Credits"
  4. Enter the amount and reason
  5. Changes are logged for audit purposes

Use cases: Complimentary credits, corrections, promotions, expired credit adjustments.

👥 Managing Members

View Members: Admin Dashboard > Members tab

Member Info Shown:

  • Name and email
  • Credits remaining (color-coded)
  • Account status
  • Join date

Actions:

  • View - See full profile details

  • Total Classes: Classes conducted

  • Check-ins Today: Members checked in today
