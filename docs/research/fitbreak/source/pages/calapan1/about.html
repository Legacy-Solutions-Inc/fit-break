Meet Iren: The Woman Behind FitBreak

Iren San Buenaventura is a certified personal trainer, body transformation specialist, and one of the few certified women's fitness coaches in the industry.

Her journey into fitness began long before FitBreak was founded. As a woman, professional, and mental health advocate, Iren deeply understands the struggle of balancing work, family, and personal health. She is also very personally aware of how conscious women can be about their bodies.

She started FitBreak to become the safe space she wished existed years ago—a women-only fitness studio where every woman can feel safe, be empowered, and get stronger physically and mentally.

Today, FitBreak stands as a reflection of Iren's philosophy:
"Strength is not just physical—it is emotional, mental, and deeply personal."
