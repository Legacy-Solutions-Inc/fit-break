Club House Rules

A safe, empowering, and respectful space for every woman.

1

No Class Video Recording

Videos during classes or coaching sessions are not allowed. Let's honor everyone's comfort and privacy.

2

Women's Space Only

Male companions (husbands, boyfriends, brothers, etc.) may not wait inside. They're welcome to relax at the coffee shop downstairs.

3

Selfies Allowed — Respect Privacy

Feel free to take selfies, but no photos or videos of other members without their permission.

4

Late? Please Ask the Coach First

If you arrive late, kindly check with the Coach before joining the class to avoid disruptions.

5

No Food Inside the Club

Drinks are allowed. Keep the space clean and fresh for everyone.

6

Watch Your Belongings

The club is not responsible for lost items. Please safeguard your personal belongings.

7

Payment Required Before Joining Classes

Only paid members may join classes. Settle payments in advance to enjoy uninterrupted sessions.

8

Respectful Behavior Only

Unruly or disruptive behavior may result in removal from the premises.

9

Return Equipment After Use

Use equipment safely and return everything to its proper place.

10

Proper Attire & Hygiene

Wear appropriate workout clothes and clean shoes. Maintain good hygiene to keep our community comfortable.

Thank you for helping keep FitBreak a safe, supportive, and empowering space for all women!
